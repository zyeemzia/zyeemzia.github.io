1. Introduction

Welcome to Military Gadget. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our tactical field suite — GPS tracker, guided route follow with voice, waypoints, compass and clinometer, timers, sun and moon planning, field calculators with resection, LED signalling, report formats, and corps planning tools. By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

Military Gadget has no sign-in and no user accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

  • Precise Location Data (GPS): Location access powers the core features: showing your position and grid reference (MGRS), recording tracks, guiding you along a route, and the field calculators. Your positions, tracks, routes, and waypoints are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Tracking With the Screen Off: When you start a track or a guided route, the app keeps receiving location through an Android foreground service, shown by a persistent notification for as long as it runs. The app does not request "all the time" background location, and location stops when you stop or pause tracking or guidance.
  • Your Field Data Stays On Your Phone: Saved routes, waypoints and targets, report drafts (SALUTE, MEDEVAC, LOCSTAT, SITREP), settings, and downloaded offline map areas are stored locally on your device. An unsaved track is also checkpointed locally so it can be restored after an unexpected shutdown. This data leaves your device only when you deliberately share, export, or back it up — for example a route file or report text sent through the Android share sheet to an app you choose.
  • Device Sensors: The compass, clinometer, and orientation features read the magnetometer and accelerometer in real time. Sensor readings are used on the device and are never stored or transmitted.
  • Flashlight & Signalling: The LED signalling and torch features switch the phone's flashlight through the system torch. The app does not request the Camera permission and never takes photos or video. It does not use the microphone.
  • Voice Guidance: Spoken directions use your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. The text spoken (for example "turn left in 50 metres") is generated on the device.
  • Battery & Screen: The app reads the battery level on the device to warn you before it runs low, and keeps the screen awake during guidance if you enable it in Settings.
  • Local Notifications: The notification permission is used to show the tracking/guidance status notification required by Android. We do not track or transmit your interaction with notifications.
  • Device Identifiers: Our third-party advertising and subscription providers (below) collect device and advertising identifiers to serve ads and verify purchases.

3. Map, Place Search & Location Services

Maps and searches that need the internet contact third-party services directly from your device. We do not see or store these requests.

  • Map Tiles: Online map layers are loaded from the provider you select (Esri, OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, CyclOSM, or USGS). As with any web map, the provider receives your IP address and the map area being viewed. Areas you download for offline use are stored on your device and then work with no connection at all.
  • Place-Name Search: When you search for a place by name, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching locations. Grid references and coordinates you type are decoded entirely on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • Area Names: To show a readable area name for a position, the app may use your phone's built-in geocoding service, which sends the coordinates to the platform provider (on most Android phones, Google).

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide monetization, update, and subscription services, we integrate with the following industry-standard providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: Free users see advertisements to keep the base app free. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate IP-based location, app interaction, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent dialog before any personalized ads load. Your precise GPS location is never shared with advertisers by the app.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: The optional "Pro" upgrade (ad removal) is purchased through Google Play Billing. RevenueCat receives an anonymized app user ID and purchase history to verify your Pro entitlement. We never see, process, or store your payment card details.
  • Google Play In-App Updates: We use Google Play's native in-app update API to prompt you when a newer version is available. This only checks version metadata with Google Play; no personal data is involved.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • No Account Data: Because Military Gadget operates entirely without user accounts, we hold no personal profile data on any remote server.
  • Local Data Deletion: Routes, waypoints, reports, settings, and offline map areas can be deleted inside the app at any time, or all at once by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • No Deletion Request Required: Because we do not collect or transmit personal user data to our own servers, no formal data-deletion request to the developer is necessary.

6. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use general-purpose usage tracking, behavioral analytics, or crash-analytics SDKs of our own. Our third-party partners (AdMob and RevenueCat) collect only the minimum telemetry required to serve ads and confirm your Pro subscription status.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Consent

  • Location Control: You can deny or revoke location access at any time in Android Settings. Features that do not need a position (timers, calculators, signalling, report formats) keep working.
  • Consent Management: Where applicable (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a consent dialog is shown on launch, letting you grant or deny personalized ad tracking.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization), or reset your advertising ID from the same menu.

8. Children's Privacy

Military Gadget is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so it can be removed.

9. Security of Data

Because your positions, tracks, routes, and reports are never transmitted to our servers, the risk of a server-side data breach is mitigated by the app's architecture itself. Data exchanged with map, ad, and billing providers is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic storage or transmission is ever 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Safety Notice

Navigation, grid, and planning figures are aids only. GPS accuracy, compass readings, and map data can be wrong. Always cross-check with approved maps, instruments, and your unit's procedures before relying on any result.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal or feature changes. Any changes are reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Please review this policy periodically.
