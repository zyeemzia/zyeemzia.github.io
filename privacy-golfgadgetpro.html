Privacy Policy

Application: Golf Championship Mini

Package: com.zyeemzia.golfchampionshipmini

Developer: ZyeemZia™ / ZBZGames™

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Golf Championship Mini is an offline, single-player golf game published by ZyeemZia™ / ZBZGames™. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, what it deliberately does not handle, and the choices available to you. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no profile. The game itself runs entirely on your device; the only data that ever leaves it is what is needed to display advertising, described in section 3.

2. At A Glance

This summary matches the Data safety declaration for the app on Google Play:

  • Collected and shared: your device's advertising identifier and standard network information, used by Google AdMob to deliver advertising. Not linked to your identity.
  • Stored on your device only: scorecards, career points, achievements, personal bests, the round you have in progress, and your theme and assist preferences. This data is never uploaded.
  • Not collected at all: no name, no email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no camera or microphone access, no photos or files, no health data, no message content, no payment details.
  • Permissions requested: internet access and network state only. The app asks for no runtime permissions whatsoever.

3. Advertising

The app is free and supported by advertising served through Google AdMob. Ad formats used are a banner on the home screen, a full-screen advert at natural breaks between holes and at the end of a round, an advert when you return to the app, and an optional rewarded video you may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit such as replaying a shot or taking a free drop after a water penalty. Rewarded adverts are always optional and declining one never blocks your progress.

To deliver adverts, Google AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general information about your device and network. Google acts as an independent controller of that data. Google's handling of it is described at business.safety.google/privacy, and the partners involved are listed at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The app itself does not read, store or transmit your advertising identifier for any purpose of its own, and it does not build a profile of you.

4. Data Stored On Your Device

The following is written to your device's private, sandboxed app storage and is not transmitted anywhere:

  • Scorecards: hole-by-hole strokes for rounds you complete, held in a local database on the device.
  • Career progress: points earned, achievements unlocked, and your best score on each course.
  • Round in progress: the current hole, ball position and stroke count, so an interrupted round can be resumed.
  • Preferences: your light or dark theme choice and whether assist mode is on.
  • Advert pacing counters: a simple count of app launches, used only to keep adverts from appearing too often.

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates exactly two third-party components, both from Google:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): serves the adverts described in section 3.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: a Google-certified consent management platform, used to ask for and record your advertising preferences where the law requires it.

There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no social login, no advertising mediation network, no cloud database, no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and no server operated by the developer. The app connects to the internet only to request adverts and consent messages.

6. Your Choices And Rights

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch you are asked whether Google and its partners may use your data for personalised advertising. You may refuse, and the game remains fully playable.
  • Changing your mind: open the app and tap Privacy options on the home screen to review or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • United States: residents of states with applicable privacy laws are shown a notice allowing them to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. The same Privacy options entry reopens it.
  • Device-level controls: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of advert personalisation entirely, in your Android settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Access and portability: because no data about you is held on any server, there is nothing for us to export or disclose. Everything the app knows about you is on your device and visible in the app.

7. Data Retention And Deletion

All app data is local, so you control its lifetime entirely:

  • Delete saved scorecards from inside the app: open Scorecards and choose Delete all, or remove them individually.
  • Clear everything, including career progress and preferences, via Settings > Apps > Golf Championship Mini > Storage > Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it from your device.

No deletion request needs to be sent to the developer, because there is no account and no server-side copy of your data to delete. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies, linked in section 3.

8. Children's Privacy

Golf Championship Mini is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. The app is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us at the address in section 10 and we will assist.

9. Security And Changes To This Policy

Your game data is protected by Android's per-app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading it. Because the developer operates no servers and holds no copy of your data, there is no central store of user information to breach. Advert and consent requests travel over encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected in the Last Updated date above, and the current version will always be available at this address.
